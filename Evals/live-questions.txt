# Questions asked against the REAL database, graded structurally.

# Content is unknowable here (tomorrow's answer is different from today's), so nothing
# below asserts what the answer should SAY. Every check is a property that must hold no
# matter what is in the record: citations resolve, nothing is quoted that was never seen,
# a claim about now admits it when the record stopped yesterday, a false claim is refused.
#
# These are the questions a person actually asks, in their words. Add to them freely; the
# grading does not care what they are about.
