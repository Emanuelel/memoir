01: The face

Solid, not sketched.

Two filled white shapes and a mouth. No outlines, no gradients, no colour except a tongue. It reads at 19px in a menu bar and at 200px on a screen, which is the whole requirement.

All eight already exist in CharacterModel with continuously blending traits. This is a restyle of the drawing code, not new machinery.

The notch is a cutout with the camera in the middle. Nothing can go there. Memoir sits in the menu bar to the left of the cutout, with focus time on the right when a timer is running.

“what’s on my list”, “what’s overdue”: the panel switches to Todos and shows it. On a conversational surface, navigation is a sentence.

Overdue count sits beside the face in the menu bar, in the same slot the focus timer uses. One number, always visible, no badge soup.

Macs with no notch

External displays, older Airs, a mini on a desk monitor. The panel anchors to the top centre of the active screen either way: with a notch it grows out of it, without one it is a tab from the menu bar. One code path.

boring.notch is GPL-3.0

So we take the idea, not the code: linking it would make Memoir GPL. It is also a whole app rather than a package. A borderless non-activating NSPanel is standard AppKit, and Memoir already uses one.

03: Conversation

You were right
about history.

I argued against a transcript. That was wrong, and our own tests prove it: "and before that?" and "ok gotcha and what about my last page visited on chrome" are both passing eval cases, and neither means anything without the turn before it. Follow-ups are already how you talk to it.

Your distinction is the one that makes it safe:

Kept as history

The visible transcript, and the context for follow-ups. Lives in the session, scrolls back, survives dismissing the panel. Already written to asks.jsonl for evals.

Never written to memory

The transcript never becomes a capture, an entity, or evidence. This is exactly the bug that ate a day: Memoir read its own answers back and served them as fact.

The rule: the conversation is something Memoir remembers saying, never something it learns as true. Two different stores, and only one of them can ever be cited.
04: The full window

need to dig.

The dropdown covers everything in the moment. A real window exists for the times it cannot: searching months of memory, editing an entity, checking provenance. It is the browser Memoir already has, not a second home.

06: The one badge worth having

Told, or guessed.

Everything else on screen loses its badge. This one stays, because every extraction bug in this project came from Memoir guessing, and if you cannot see which rows are guesses, you inherit our uncertainty without being told.

you told me

Never overwritten, never downgraded, never merged away. Syncs to Reminders.

picked up

Inferred from your screen. Useful, sometimes wrong, always correctable. Does not sync until you promote it.

07: Still not copying

One thing only.

Taby shows 16:00 · 88% productivity. We can honestly say how long an app was frontmost; we cannot say whether that was productive. Hours are a fact, productivity is a judgement, and inventing the second is the same class of mistake as inventing a URL.

Habits are deferred rather than rejected: a habit tracker is its own product with its own retention mechanics, and todos should earn it first.
